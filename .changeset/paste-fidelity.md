Rich text pasted from Word or a web page keeps more: a table's rows become paragraphs of their cells' words rather than all of them run together, numbered lists keep where they start and which way they count, and code keeps its language. Pictures pasted into text, which it can't hold, are left out with a note saying to paste them outside the text, where they become blocks. A picture pasted from the clipboard as a file, which did nothing, opens the media library to upload it, and becomes an Image block once it's chosen.
